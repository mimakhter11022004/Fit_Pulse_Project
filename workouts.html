Your workouts

Four simple workouts. Rest 30 to 60 seconds between sets.

Full Body Basics

30 minutes, beginner

  • Squats - 3 x 12
  • Push-ups - 3 x 10
  • Lunges - 3 x 10 each leg
  • Plank - 3 x 30 seconds

Upper Body Strength

40 minutes, intermediate

  • Push-ups - 4 x 12
  • Dumbbell rows - 3 x 12
  • Shoulder press - 3 x 10
  • Biceps curls - 3 x 12

Lower Body Power

40 minutes, intermediate

  • Squats - 4 x 12
  • Lunges - 3 x 12 each leg
  • Glute bridges - 3 x 15
  • Calf raises - 4 x 15

Cardio Burn

25 minutes, all levels

  • Jumping jacks - 40 seconds
  • High knees - 40 seconds
  • Burpees - 30 seconds
  • Mountain climbers - 40 seconds
  • Repeat everything 4 times
