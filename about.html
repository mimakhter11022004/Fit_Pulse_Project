About FitPulse

One place for workouts, nutrition and healthy habits.

Most people do not fail because they lack motivation. They fail because fitness feels scattered: one app for workouts, another for food, a notebook for numbers.

FitPulse puts simple tools side by side. Check your BMI, find your daily calories, pick a diet plan and follow a weekly workout schedule.

What we believe

Personal

Your goal and your body are different from everyone else's, so your plan should be too.

Simple

Clear pages and short forms. Spend your time training, not learning an app.

Consistent

Small steps every day add up to real progress over time.
